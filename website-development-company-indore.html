FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in Indore?

It depends on the number of pages, features (like e-commerce or booking) and content needed. Book a 30-minute intro call, tell us what you need, and we’ll send a fixed proposal.

How long does it take to build a website?

A focused landing page can go live quickly; a full business website or store takes longer depending on pages and content. We share a timeline with every proposal.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. Every site ships with technical SEO, structured data, a sitemap and fast performance — and it’s ready for AI search engines too.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We can build on a platform you can edit, or manage updates for you as part of website management.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We redesign while preserving your URLs and rankings so you don’t lose existing Google traffic.

Do you provide website management after launch?

Yes — updates, security, backups, speed checks and content changes every month.
