## 4. What was implemented (on-site)
**SEO — 15 new pages + hub, each targeting one cluster:**
- /chatgpt-ads-agency-india · /reddit-ads-agency-india · /meta-ads-agency-indore · /google-ads-agency-indore
- /social-media-marketing-agency-indore · /influencer-marketing-agency-indore · /ugc-video-production-agency
- /branding-agency-indore · /website-development-company-indore · /digital-marketing-agency-vijay-nagar-indore
- /case-studies/meta-ads-30k-52-enquiries · /about
- /insights/ (hub) · /insights/chatgpt-ads-india-guide · /insights/reddit-ads-cost-india · /insights/meta-ads-cost-india
- Unique title (≤60) and description (≤160) per page, one H1, canonical URL, breadcrumbs, Open Graph.
- Internal linking: homepage service cards, Reddit/ChatGPT cards and footer link to every page; every page links to 4 related pages.
- Sitemap updated (18 URLs), Search Console-ready.

**AEO (answer engines, featured snippets, voice):**
- "Quick answer" box (40–60 words) at the top of every page — the direct answer first.
- 3–6 FAQs per page in question form, marked up as FAQPage.
- Facts tables (definitions, costs, dates) that snippets can lift cleanly.
- "Key takeaways" summary on every guide.

**GEO (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews):**
- `/llms.txt` + `/llms-full.txt` — a machine-readable summary of who we are, key facts, every page and FAQ.
- `robots.txt` explicitly allows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Meta-ExternalAgent.
- An entity page (/about) with an "at a glance" facts table: legal name, founder, address, services, clients, results.
- Statistics + citations + sourced claims on every guide — the tactics the Princeton GEO study (Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024) found raise AI visibility by up to ~40%.
- Consistent naming (Akarsa Studio), address and phone everywhere (NAP consistency).
- Service, Article, Breadcrumb, FAQ and Organization structured data on every page.
