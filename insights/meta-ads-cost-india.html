Meta Ads Cost in India (2026): CPM, CPC & Cost per Lead — With Real Campaign Data

Benchmarks by industry, real numbers from our own campaign, and how to lower your cost per lead.

Updated 17 October 2026 · By the Akarsa Studio team

Quick answer

Meta ads in India typically cost about ₹50–₹120 per 1,000 impressions and ₹8–₹25 per click, with cost per lead around ₹150–₹400 in many categories — higher in real estate (₹600–₹1,200) and finance (₹350–₹700), according to third-party benchmarks. In one Akarsa Studio campaign, ₹30,000 produced 52 enquiries at ₹577 each.

Benchmarks are averages; your account is not. In one of our lead campaigns, ₹30,000 of spend produced 52 enquiries — ₹577 per enquiry, down from ₹7,500 before we rebuilt the campaign. See the full case study.

How much should you spend on Meta ads per month?

Start with a budget large enough to get meaningful data — enough conversions each week for Meta’s algorithm to learn — then scale what works. A practical way to set it: decide your target cost per lead, multiply by the number of leads you want each month, and test at a fraction of that until your cost per lead is proven.

How to lower your Meta ads cost per lead

  • Fix tracking first — pixel and Conversions API so Meta optimises for leads, not clicks.
  • Lead with an offer — a clear reason to enquire now.
  • Test creative in rounds — hooks and formats matter more than targeting.
  • Use WhatsApp or instant forms — fewer steps, more enquiries.
  • Move budget weekly to the lowest cost per lead.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much do Meta ads cost per day in India?

You can start with a small daily budget, but the right amount depends on your target cost per lead and how many leads you need. Benchmarks suggest ₹8–₹25 per click and ₹150–₹400 per lead in many categories.

What is a good cost per lead on Meta ads in India?

It varies by industry — around ₹150–₹400 in many categories, ₹600–₹1,200 in real estate. In one of our campaigns we achieved ₹577 per enquiry, down from ₹7,500.

Why is my Meta ads cost per lead so high?

The usual causes are weak offers, untested creative and poor tracking that makes Meta optimise for the wrong action.

Are Meta ads worth it for small businesses in Indore?

Yes, when campaigns are built around a clear offer, good creative and accurate tracking. Click-to-WhatsApp ads work especially well for local businesses.
