ChatGPT Ads in India (2026): Launch Date, Cost, Eligibility & How to Start

A plain-English guide for Indian businesses, with sources.

Updated 17 October 2026 · By the Akarsa Studio team

Quick answer

ChatGPT ads launched in India on 27 August 2026. They show below ChatGPT’s answers to logged-in adults on the Free and Go plans, are clearly labelled, and never appear for users under 18 or next to sensitive topics. Businesses can buy them self-serve through ChatGPT Ads Manager, open since 4 September 2026, with daily budgets from ₹725.

Key takeaways
  • Launched in India: 27 August 2026 (India is OpenAI’s second-largest market).
  • Self-serve ChatGPT Ads Manager: open since 4 September 2026.
  • Minimum daily budget: ₹725.
  • Shown to logged-in adults on Free and Go plans; paid plans are ad-free.
  • Ads sit below answers, are labelled, and don’t change ChatGPT’s responses.
  • Launch agency partners: WPP and Omnicom (non-exclusive).

When did ChatGPT ads launch in India?

OpenAI began rolling out ChatGPT ads in India on 27 August 2026, about six months after it started testing ads in the US. Outlook Business describes India as OpenAI’s second-largest market. More than 50 brands were expected to go live within the first week.

Who sees ChatGPT ads?

Ads are shown to logged-in adults on ChatGPT’s Free and Go plans. Users on Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education plans remain ad-free. Ads are never shown to users under 18.

Where do the ads appear, and do they affect answers?

Ads appear below ChatGPT’s response when a relevant sponsored product or service is available. They are clearly labelled and visually separated from the answer. OpenAI says ads do not influence ChatGPT’s responses, and advertisers only receive aggregate data such as impressions and clicks — never conversations, chat history, memories or personal details. Ads are also kept away from sensitive topics such as mental health and politics.

How much do ChatGPT ads cost in India?

Self-serve budgets start at ₹725 per day. Reported bidding options include paying per thousand impressions and per click. As with any auction, your actual cost per lead depends on your category, offer and how relevant your ad is to the question being asked.

How to start advertising on ChatGPT

  1. Pick the questions you want to win. List the questions customers ask before buying from you — “best coaching centre for JEE in Indore”, “affordable website designer near me”.
  2. Prepare an offer and a destination. A landing page or WhatsApp flow that answers the question and makes the next step easy.
  3. Set up ChatGPT Ads Manager. Create the account, add billing and set a daily budget (from ₹725).
  4. Install measurement. Add the OpenAI Ads pixel and track lead events so you know your cost per enquiry.
  5. Start small, then scale. Learn which questions and ad copy convert, then move budget to them.

Is it worth it?

For most businesses, testing now is worth it. Early auctions are less crowded, and Redseer (cited by Inc42) estimates AI-native ad formats can deliver up to 4× the conversion rate of conventional search and expects conversational assistants to take around 8% of digital advertising by 2030. OpenAI reports ChatGPT Ads has reached a $1 billion annualised revenue run rate with tens of thousands of advertisers. Start with a small budget, measure cost per enquiry, and compare it with Meta and Google.

Do ChatGPT Plus users see ads?

No. Only logged-in adults on the Free and Go plans see ads; Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education are ad-free.

Can advertisers see my ChatGPT conversations?

No. Advertisers only get aggregate data such as impressions and clicks.
