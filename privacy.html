Privacy Policy

Akarsa Studio Private Limited · Effective 13 October 2026

This policy explains what we collect when you contact Akarsa Studio or request a free ad teardown through one of our forms, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Akarsa Studio Private Limited is a marketing and creative agency based in Vijay Nagar, Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. This website is akarsaone.xyz.

What we collect

When you submit one of our lead forms (on LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram or this website) or message us on WhatsApp, we receive:

  • Your first and last name
  • Email address and phone number
  • Company name
  • Your answers to the questions on the form, such as your business type and approximate monthly ad spend

If you later give us access to your ad accounts or reports so we can prepare a teardown, we use that information only for that review.

This website also uses an advertising measurement tag (the OpenAI Ads pixel) to understand which ads bring visitors to us.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand which pages and films help visitors, and how well our own ads work. We measure page views, film plays, clicks on email, phone and WhatsApp, enquiries sent (only the sector and budget band you chose) and calls booked. We never send your name, email or phone number to these tools.

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept cookies. If you decline, the Meta Pixel does not load and Google Analytics runs without cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

How we use it

  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about the request you made
  • To prepare and send the review or proposal you asked for
  • To tell you about our services if you are interested; you can ask us to stop at any time

We do not sell your information and do not share it with other advertisers.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are held in the advertising platform you used (for example LinkedIn Campaign Manager or Meta Ads Manager) and in our internal lead records, which only our team can access.

How long we keep it

We keep lead details for up to 12 months after our last contact with you, or less if you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. You can also withdraw any consent you have given.
