Quick answer

Akarsa Studio (Akarsa Studio Private Limited) is an AI-driven marketing agency based in Vijay Nagar, Indore, India, founded by Ritik Sharma. It offers social media marketing, UGC and reels, influencer marketing, Meta, Google, YouTube, Reddit and ChatGPT ads, branding and website development, and has worked with 20+ brands including Starbucks, Adidas and Decathlon.
