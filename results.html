No mock-ups and no rounded-up claims. Every figure below comes from Meta Ads Manager, with the dates, the spend and the maths shown.

Quick answer

For an Indian e-commerce brand, Akarsa Studio’s Meta ads produced 196 purchases from ₹74,600 of ad spend in 30 days (6 Sep–7 Oct 2026): ₹381 per purchase and a 3.7× average ROAS. On our own lead campaigns, Meta reports 186 leads, with our best campaigns at ₹28.68 and ₹47.76 per lead.

Frequently asked questions

What ROAS do Akarsa Studio’s Meta ads deliver?

For an Indian e-commerce brand: a 3.7× average purchase ROAS over 30 days (6 Sep–7 Oct 2026), with 196 purchases from ₹74,600 of spend, or ₹381 per purchase. For an international store: 2.74× ROAS ($419 of ads → $1,149 in purchases). Results depend on your product, margins and offer, so we never promise fixed numbers.
