Real Meta Ads Results, in Numbers

No mock-ups and no rounded-up claims. Every figure below comes from Meta Ads Manager, with the dates, the spend and the maths shown.

Updated 21 October 2026 · By the Akarsa Studio team

Quick answer

For an Indian e-commerce brand, Akarsa Studio’s Meta ads produced 196 purchases from ₹74,600 of ad spend over 32 days (6 Sep–7 Oct 2026): ₹381 per purchase and a 3.7× total ROAS, about ₹2.74 lakh in tracked sales. For an international store, the same approach returned 2.74× ROAS.

Maths check: ₹74,600 ÷ 196 purchases = ₹381 per purchase. Each ad’s spend × its ROAS, added up, comes to about ₹2.74 lakh of tracked sales: ₹2.74 lakh ÷ ₹74,600 = 3.68× total ROAS, shown rounded as 3.7×. Reach is shown per ad and not added up, because the same people can see more than one ad.
