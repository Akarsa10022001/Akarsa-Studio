FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does logo design cost in Indore?

It depends on whether you need a logo only or a complete brand identity with guidelines. Share your brief on WhatsApp and we’ll send a proposal.

What’s included in a brand identity?

Typically a logo suite, colour palette, typography, graphic elements, imagery direction and a brand guidelines document, plus templates for social media.

How many revisions do I get?

Our Brand Launch package includes two revision rounds, so you can refine the direction until it feels right.

Which files will I receive?

Vector and web-ready files of your logo in every colour version, plus your colour and font specifications and guidelines.

Do you do rebranding?

Yes. We refresh or rebuild identities while keeping the recognition you’ve already earned.

Can you design packaging and social media templates?

Yes — packaging, menus, brochures and ready-to-use social media templates that match your new identity.
