What is UGC content?

UGC (user-generated content) is video or photo content that looks and feels like it was made by a real customer — a creator talking to camera, an unboxing, a review — rather than a polished ad. Brands use UGC on their own pages and in paid ads because it feels native to the feed and builds trust faster than traditional creative.

Hook-first, made for how people scroll

Every video we make starts with the hook: the first second decides whether anyone watches the rest. We script hooks, test variations, and edit for pace, captions and sound — the same craft behind our own reels like “Your marketing is a lie” and “Posting ≠ Growth”.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is UGC content?

UGC (user-generated content) is content that looks like it was made by a real customer — reviews, unboxings, talking-to-camera videos. Brands use it organically and in ads because it feels authentic and builds trust.

How much does UGC video production cost in India?

It depends on the number of videos, whether creators appear on camera, shooting needs and usage rights. We create a plan around your monthly content needs.

Do you provide the creators?

Yes. We source and brief creators who match your audience, or we can work with your team on camera.

Can you edit our existing footage?

Yes. Video editing is a standalone service — send your raw footage and we’ll turn it into reels and ads.

How many reels can you produce per month?

Volume is set in your plan. We recommend a sustainable cadence with enough variations to test hooks.

Who owns the content and usage rights?

Usage rights are agreed upfront in every project, including paid-ad usage for creator content.
