How much do Meta ads cost in India?

Third-party benchmarks put Meta ads in India at roughly ₹50–₹120 CPM, ₹8–₹25 per click and ₹150–₹400 per lead for many categories — with real estate (₹600–₹1,200) and finance (₹350–₹700) costing more. Your cost depends on industry, offer, creative and audience. See our detailed Meta ads cost in India guide.

Frequently asked questions

What results can Meta ads deliver?

In one of our campaigns, ₹30,000 of spend produced 52 enquiries and cut cost per enquiry from ₹7,500 to ₹577. Results vary by industry and offer, which is why we start with an audit.

How much do Meta ads cost in India?

Third-party benchmarks suggest about ₹50–₹120 per 1,000 impressions, ₹8–₹25 per click and ₹150–₹400 per lead in many categories. Competitive industries like real estate and finance cost more.

What are your Meta ads service charges?

Do you create the ad creative?

Can you run WhatsApp ads?
