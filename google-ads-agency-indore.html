FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much do Google Ads cost in Indore?

You pay per click, and the price depends on how competitive each keyword is. We forecast costs during research using Google’s keyword data, then set a budget around your target cost per lead.

Google Ads or Meta ads — which is better?

Google captures existing demand from people searching; Meta creates demand by reaching people as they scroll. Most businesses get the best results using both, with budget shifted to whichever delivers cheaper enquiries.

How fast do Google Ads bring leads?

Search campaigns can bring enquiries within days of launch. The first few weeks are spent refining keywords and bids to bring the cost per lead down.

Do you run YouTube ads?

Yes — YouTube in-stream and Shorts ads for awareness and retargeting, using creative our team produces.

Can you target only my area of Indore?

Yes. Google Ads supports location targeting down to a radius around your business, so you only pay for searches from people who can actually visit or order.

Will you set up conversion tracking?

Always. We track calls, form fills and WhatsApp clicks so you can see exactly what each rupee returns.
