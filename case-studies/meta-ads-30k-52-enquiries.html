Meta Ads Case Study: ₹30K → 52 Enquiries

One lead-generation campaign, two numbers that matter: what it cost, and what it returned.

Updated 17 October 2026 · By the Akarsa Studio team

Quick answer

In this Meta ads lead-generation campaign, Akarsa Studio turned ₹30,000 of ad spend into 52 enquiries. Cost per enquiry fell from ₹7,500 to ₹577 — about 13 times lower — because the campaign was rebuilt around offers, creative and tracking rather than boosting posts.

Maths check: ₹30,000 ÷ 52 enquiries = ₹577 per enquiry. ₹7,500 ÷ ₹577 ≈ 13.

Why the cost per enquiry fell so far

A ₹7,500 cost per enquiry usually means money is going to the wrong people, the offer isn’t compelling, or Meta is optimising for the wrong signal. The fix isn’t a bigger budget — it’s a better system. Our standard rebuild for lead campaigns covers:

  • Offer clarity — one clear reason to enquire now.
  • Creative in rounds — several hooks and formats tested against each other.
  • Signal quality — pixel and conversion tracking so Meta optimises for real enquiries, not cheap clicks.
  • Budget discipline — spend moved to the ads with the lowest cost per enquiry every week.
