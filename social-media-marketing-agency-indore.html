FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does social media marketing cost in Indore?

It depends on the platforms, how many reels and posts you need each month, whether we shoot on location, and your ad budget. We offer tailored monthly plans — message us on WhatsApp with your goals and we’ll send a proposal.

How many posts and reels will I get each month?

We set the volume in your plan based on your goals and budget. Consistency matters more than volume, so we prioritise a calendar you can sustain with content that performs.

Do you shoot content or do we need to provide it?

We handle everything — strategy, scripting, shooting, editing and posting. If you already have photos or videos, we can improve and repurpose them.

Which social media platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and Shorts, LinkedIn, X and Pinterest. We recommend the right mix for your audience.

How long before social media marketing shows results?

Most brands see early traction in the first one to three months. Lasting growth comes from consistency, testing and doubling down on what your audience loves.

Do you also run social media ads?

Yes. We pair organic content with Meta ads so your best-performing posts reach more of the right people — see our Meta ads agency page.
