Why advertise on ChatGPT now

People don’t scroll past ChatGPT answers — they ask a specific question and act on what they read. That makes every placement high-intent. Three numbers explain the opportunity:

  • Early window. Only about 50 brands were expected to go live in India in the launch week, so auctions are far less crowded than Meta or Google.
  • Higher conversion. Redseer estimates AI-native ad formats can deliver up to 4× the conversion rate of conventional search ads (as reported by Inc42).
  • Where budgets are heading. Redseer expects conversational AI assistants to account for around 8% of digital advertising by 2030.

OpenAI says ChatGPT Ads has already reached a $1 billion annualised revenue run rate with tens of thousands of advertisers worldwide. The brands that learn the format now will own the cheapest clicks while competition is still low.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are ChatGPT ads available in India?

Yes. OpenAI rolled out ChatGPT ads in India on 27 August 2026, and the self-serve ChatGPT Ads Manager opened to Indian businesses on 4 September 2026.

How much do ChatGPT ads cost in India?

Daily budgets start at ₹725. What you actually pay per lead depends on your category, offer and targeting — we start close to the minimum, measure cost per enquiry, then scale what works.

Who sees ChatGPT ads?

Logged-in adults on ChatGPT’s Free and Go plans. Users on Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education plans don’t see ads, and ads are never shown to anyone under 18.

Do ChatGPT ads change ChatGPT’s answers?

No. OpenAI says ads don’t influence responses. They appear below the answer, clearly labelled and visually separated, and advertisers never get access to conversations.

Can a small business in Indore run ChatGPT ads?

Yes. With self-serve buying from ₹725 a day, a café, gym, clinic or coaching centre can test ChatGPT ads without an enterprise budget. Local intent questions are often the cheapest to win.

Why use an agency for ChatGPT ads?

The format is new, so most teams are still guessing. We bring conversational copywriting, offer design, lead tracking and weekly optimisation — and we already run performance campaigns on Meta, Google and Reddit.
